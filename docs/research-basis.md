# Research basis and limits

Reviewed on 9 October 2026. Questions in the new engine are original authored templates, not copied past-paper questions. Document contents were treated as source material, not instructions.

## Assessment and curriculum

- [Cambridge Primary Checkpoint](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-primary/assessment/cambridge-primary-checkpoint/): assessment context; this app is independent practice, not endorsed by Cambridge.
- Cambridge-authored Stage 6 framework copies: [mathematics](https://aiisgambia.org/assets/curriculum/Year6maths.pdf), [science](https://www.pea.ae/linkfiles/Science_Primary_Framework/Stage6.pdf), [English](https://aiisgambia.org/assets/curriculum/Year6english.pdf). Objective tags guide scope but are not proof of complete coverage or difficulty calibration.
- Supplied 0096 mathematics 2023 Paper 1 and 0097 science October 2024 Paper 2 were inspected for question wording, diagrams and paper layout. Their covers specify 40 marks; maths has 45 minutes and science 35. Half-paper practice therefore targets 20 marks and half that time.
- Supplied 0058 English 2025 Paper 1 mark scheme and insert informed passage-based reading, separate writing and a 25-mark writing rubric. The source's short-response caps are applied to self-assessed writing. Five criteria are creation (5), vocabulary (3), structure (7), grammar/punctuation (7), spelling (3).
- Supplied mathematics 2025/2026 and science 2024 mark schemes were extracted as supporting references; rotated/table extraction was imperfect. No claim is made that every marking cell was reliably parsed.

Supplied filenames sometimes say May while the document itself says April. Use the internal paper identification when checking sources. Additional online paper searches were made, but inaccessible PDF results are not counted as fully reviewed papers. This is not an exhaustive multi-year paper review.

## Product ideas

- [exam-mate](https://www.exam-mate.com/index.php): topic and paper filtering informed the setup controls.
- [Khan Academy skills-to-proficient](https://blog.khanacademy.org/why-khan-academy-will-be-using-skills-to-proficient-to-measure-learning-outcomes/) and [classroom experience](https://blog.khanacademy.org/meet-the-new-khan-academy-classroom-experience/): actionable skill feedback informed topic summaries and separated unmarked work. The app does not claim a validated mastery model.

## Child-directed AI constraint

[Gemini API additional terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) prohibit API clients directed at, or likely accessed by, people under 18 under the public terms inspected. Routing requests through a server does not resolve that eligibility restriction. The former proxy and browser-key fallback are therefore disabled rather than labelled safe. A future service requires contractual eligibility, privacy review and age-appropriate evaluation before integration.

## What still needs expert work

The collection includes original authored families, 6 original reading passages and 80 writing prompts. The October 2026 count test finds 8,533 distinct mathematics MCQ stems, 2,507 science stems and 648 English stems; structured practice has 120 distinct two-part mathematics groups, 837 science groups and 78 English passage-question parts. These are unique rendered questions, not that many independently designed templates. Numerical/context variants provide breadth. Some Hard labels still need tougher reasoning and better distractors. Ten two-mark groups are an interim maths/science half-paper structure, not the full variety of Cambridge response formats. Teacher review, more passages, drawing/plotting/matching/table responses and a complete objective-by-objective coverage audit remain necessary.
